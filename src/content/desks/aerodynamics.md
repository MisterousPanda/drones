Sparrow is an indoor trainer. We optimize for repeatable hover and calm step response, not a 4-in race duct. Rings add drag and some turbulent inflow. That is accepted.

Keep the boom lengths honest so the mixer’s roll/pitch arms stay symmetric. A bent guard that leans into one prop is an unmodeled yaw moment. Replace the ring before you retune PID.

First flights stay in a room or a cage. Outdoor wind is a later notebook.

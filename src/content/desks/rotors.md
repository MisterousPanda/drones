Three-inch two-blade plastics match the 1404 3700 Kv motors without asking for a 4-in race prop’s current. Full rings stay on for V1. They cost a little thrust and save a lot of fingers, drywall, and gyros.

Keep the discs matched. A nicked blade is a vibration source the Mahony filter will eat as attitude error. Balance or replace. Fingers stay outside the disc even with rings — rings are a courtesy, not armor.

Never remove rings to “see if it hovers better” before the first indoor pack is boring.

245 g is a promise, not a vibe. Weigh the ready-to-fly bird with the 3S 650, rings, and XT30 leads. If it is over, do not “add a little more throttle.” Cut camera, cut a thicker frame, cut a 4-in-1 with extra capacitors you do not need.

Hover thrust for 245 g is about 2.4 N. We want near 2:1 peak thrust so a gust or a bad mixer floor does not put it on the carpet. If the scale says 280 g, the aero desk no longer owes you that margin.

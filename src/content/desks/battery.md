A 650 mAh 3S is enough energy for a 245 g indoor trainer if the hover current stays honest. Budget 50–52 g for the pack and pigtail. XT30, not XT60 — the extra plastic is just mass on this bird.

Charge in a fire bag, attended, on a hard floor. Storage voltage between sessions. A puffed cell is retired, not “flown careful.”

The LVC in `flight-core` warns at 10.5 V (3.50 V/cell) and cuts at 9.9 V (3.30 V/cell). A cut latches so a bouncing pack cannot re-arm mid-sag. Land on warn. Swap on cut.
